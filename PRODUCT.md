# CinePath — Product & Design Foundation

## 1. Product Identity
CinePath is an ultra-modern, high-performance cinema booking and digital ticketing platform. It provides film lovers with instant showtime discovery, interactive real-time seat reservation, frictionless checkout in under 90 seconds, and verified cryptographic digital ticket passes.

## 2. Target Audience & Brand Persona
- **Audience:** Discerning moviegoers, cinema enthusiasts, and casual viewers who demand zero wait times, clear seat selection, and a premium visual experience.
- **Brand Vibe:** "Cinema Prestige" — deep obsidian dark mode, radiant crimson & warm gold cinema accents, editorial typography, atmospheric projection lighting, and tactile micro-interactions.

## 3. Surface Modes (Impeccable Standards)
- **Persuade & Experience (Home, Movie Details, Cinemas):** Immersive, cinematic, high visual impact. Full commitment to mood, rich TMDB backdrops, authentic cinema badges (IMAX, Dolby Atmos, 4DX).
- **Operate (Seat Selection, Checkout, Admin/Staff Scanner):** Laser-focused clarity, ultra-crisp contrast, zero cognitive clutter, tactile seat maps, instant price updates, transparent steps.
- **Delight (Ticket Pass Receipt):** Physical ticket aesthetic with digital superpower — perforated stubs, holographic cinema seal, high-density QR code, and instant pass printing.
